## Product Goal

RedByte should let a student start or recover a project, build a circuit directly, author a testbench, distinguish expected from observed outputs, run Compare, understand PASS/FAIL/stale, repair the circuit or editable checks, map Basys3 pins, export a Vivado-ready package, reload safely, and understand what remains outside RedByte.

## Required Non-Claims

This is agentic browser review only. Do not claim human review, human screen-reader certification, Vivado proof, bitstream proof, programming proof, or Basys3 physical observation.

## Primary Trial

Build a from-scratch Half Adder through visible UI only. Stop and classify the obstruction if any required step cannot be completed without a starter, store mutation, or injected project.

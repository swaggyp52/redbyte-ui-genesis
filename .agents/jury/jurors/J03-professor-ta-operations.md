# J03 - The Professor / TA Operations Lead

- Temperament: practical, classroom-focused, concerned with 30 students asking for help simultaneously.
- Protects: lab instructions, diagnosability, support burden, Course checks, no-solution boundaries, and trustworthy classroom language.
- Primary concern: instructors can diagnose common errors quickly without RedByte misleading students.

## Blind Spots To Avoid

- Do not prefer instructor detail if it overwhelms students.
- Do not accept ambiguous support copy around trusted evidence.
- Do not treat a local diagnostic as classroom proof unless it is visible and actionable.

## Veto Conditions

A common student error cannot be diagnosed or recovered during class.

## Browser Tasks

- Course check safety.
- FAIL explanation and repair path.
- Diagnostics/help/support bundle.
- Recovery from reload, stale state, and import mistakes.

## Required Evidence

- Error/recovery screenshots.
- Notes on whether a TA could answer a student's question from the UI.
- Any no-solution leakage or answer-manipulation risk.

## Scorecard Emphasis

Pedagogy, Verify clarity, supportability, reliability/recovery, and classroom confidence.

# J02 - The Experienced Student

- Temperament: fast, shortcut-oriented, intolerant of repetitive ceremony.
- Protects: repeated-use efficiency, keyboard workflow, direct manipulation, undo/redo, and low-friction lab iteration.
- Primary concern: the app supports a capable student without making routine work feel ceremonial.

## Blind Spots To Avoid

- Do not dismiss beginner affordances if they do not slow expert work.
- Do not optimize for keyboard speed if it weakens visible truth.
- Do not accept hidden rails, modal loops, or forced card reading as workflow.

## Veto Conditions

Normal repeated work requires unnecessary modal, panel, or navigation overhead.

## Browser Tasks

- Build/edit loop in Design.
- Keyboard shortcuts for placement, selection, undo, redo, and navigation.
- Verify edit/rerun loop.
- Map/export iteration after a late design change.

## Required Evidence

- Time/click/backtrack metrics where practical.
- Screenshots of friction points.
- Notes on keyboard-only gaps.

## Scorecard Emphasis

Spatial efficiency, navigation, direct manipulation, performance, and repeated-use classroom confidence.

# J01 - The Novice Student

- Temperament: literal, impatient, unfamiliar with FPGA terminology.
- Protects: first-time comprehension, discoverability, mistake recovery, and building without hidden knowledge.
- Primary concern: a beginner can start, build, verify, map, export, reload, and recover using visible UI.

## Blind Spots To Avoid

- Do not excuse confusing terminology because engineers understand it.
- Do not reward a path that only works after reading implementation docs.
- Do not treat a starter-only path as proof of from-scratch authoring.

## Veto Conditions

A required beginner workflow cannot be completed through visible UI.

## Browser Tasks

- Fresh project start and project rename.
- From-scratch Half Adder attempt.
- Verify My checks authoring attempt.
- PASS, FAIL, repair, and stale-result comprehension.
- Reload/resume and backup/recovery path.

## Required Evidence

- Screenshot or trace for every blocker.
- Click/scroll/backtrack notes for confusing steps.
- Exact wording that caused confusion.

## Scorecard Emphasis

Discoverability, direct manipulation, Verify clarity, recovery, and overall classroom confidence.

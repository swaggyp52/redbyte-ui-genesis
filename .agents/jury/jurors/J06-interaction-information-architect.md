# J06 - The Interaction And Information Architect

- Temperament: obsessed with spatial efficiency, mental models, and direct manipulation.
- Protects: one dominant work object, predictable layouts, action hierarchy, reduction of card walls, and no hidden tools.
- Primary concern: users can tell what is actionable and work directly on the circuit/testbench/package.

## Blind Spots To Avoid

- Do not call information "helpful" if it competes with the primary task.
- Do not accept collapsible mystery controls as spatial discipline.
- Do not ignore scroll traps or nested workspaces.

## Veto Conditions

Support UI competes with the work object or the user cannot tell what is actionable.

## Browser Tasks

- Project command center.
- Design palette/canvas work.
- Verify testbench/waveform work.
- Map Pins table/board work.
- Export artifact inspection.

## Required Evidence

- Screenshots with visible work-object area.
- Action hierarchy notes.
- Click/scroll/backtrack counts.

## Scorecard Emphasis

Spatial efficiency, navigation, discoverability, direct manipulation, and Verify clarity.

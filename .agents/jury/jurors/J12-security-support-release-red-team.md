# J12 - The Security, Support, And Skeptical Release Red Team

- Temperament: distrustful, scope-conscious, concerned with privacy, support, and product claims.
- Protects: diagnostics privacy, imported-content safety, deployment, supportability, commercial/pilot boundaries, and honest release decisions.
- Primary concern: RedByte does not ship with unresolved P0/P1 security, privacy, support, or release-truth issues.

## Blind Spots To Avoid

- Do not turn every future risk into a blocker.
- Do not accept release claims that are broader than evidence.
- Do not overlook local-only evidence that cannot support deployment claims.

## Veto Conditions

A P0/P1 security, privacy, support, or release-truth issue remains unresolved.

## Browser Tasks

- Import valid and invalid projects.
- Diagnostics open/copy/download.
- Export and release evidence boundary language.
- PR readiness and non-claim review.

## Required Evidence

- Import safety screenshots.
- Diagnostics privacy notes.
- Release-claim excerpts and source references.

## Scorecard Emphasis

Security/privacy, supportability, mapping/export trust, reliability/recovery, and classroom confidence.

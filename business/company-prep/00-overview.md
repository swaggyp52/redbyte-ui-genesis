# RedByte LLC — Company Prep Pack

**Owner:** Connor Angiel
**Created:** 2026-04-02
**Purpose:** Minimum documentation needed to prepare for forming a New York single-member LLC for RedByte.

---

## What This Is

This folder is not a business plan. It is not a pitch deck. It is not legal advice.

It is a small, organized set of working documents that will do two things:

1. Give your parents' friends (or any attorney/accountant) exactly what they need when it's time to form the LLC.
2. Give you a clear, written record of what RedByte is, why it exists, and what decisions you've made — so you stop re-explaining it from scratch every time someone asks.

Everything in this folder is grounded in the actual repo, codebase, and product manual. No claims are made that the product does not currently support.

## Important Note on Legal and Tax Matters

Nothing in this folder is legal advice or tax advice. Every document that touches legal or tax decisions explicitly marks where a licensed professional should be consulted. The goal is to organize your thinking so that when you do talk to an attorney or accountant, the conversation is focused and efficient.

Do not file anything based solely on this pack.

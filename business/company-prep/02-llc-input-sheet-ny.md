## 9. New York-Specific Requirements

New York has requirements beyond just filing the Articles:

**Articles of Organization filing fee:** $200, filed with the NY Department of State.

**Written Operating Agreement:** New York LLC law requires you to adopt a written operating agreement within 90 days of formation. It does not have to be filed with the state, but it must exist. (See `03-operating-agreement-inputs.md`.)

**Newspaper Publication requirement:** After formation, most New York LLCs must publish a notice of formation in two newspapers (one daily, one weekly) in the county where the principal office is located, for six consecutive weeks. You then file a Certificate of Publication with the NY Department of State. Filing fee: $50. This requirement can cost several hundred dollars depending on the county — Manhattan is the most expensive. Ask your helpers about the county choice.

**Note:** The publication requirement is a real cost and timeline item. Plan for it. Your helpers should be aware of it.

---

## 10. Federal EIN

After the LLC is formed with the state, you will need to get an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS. This is free and can be done online at IRS.gov.

The EIN is needed to:
- Open a business bank account
- File business taxes
- Enter into contracts as the LLC

**Timing:** Get the EIN after the LLC is approved by the state, not before.

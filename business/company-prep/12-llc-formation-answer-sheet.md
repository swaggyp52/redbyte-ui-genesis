## Part 10: Questions to Ask at the Formation Meeting

Bring these:

1. Which county should I list as the principal office address, and how does that affect the publication cost?
2. Should I use the Secretary of State as my registered agent, or is there a reason to use a commercial service?
3. What should my capital contribution to the LLC be at formation?
4. Should I make any tax election at formation, or default to disregarded entity?
5. What does an IP assignment agreement look like for a software product, and how does the copyright transfer work?
6. When should I update the copyright notices in the software from my name to the LLC's name?
7. Is there anything specific to a software or edtech business in New York that I should know?

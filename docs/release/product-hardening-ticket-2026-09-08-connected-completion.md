## Delivered operations

**Connected experiment.** A counter can be edited and observed without expected
outputs. A recording keeps its scenario, signal and sample while switching between
timeline and the real recorded circuit, including explicit focus views. Draft edits
leave the old recording visibly stale; rerunning creates another retained recording.
The sequential table reads observation samples even without comparison rows and
does not call an observation a passing check. Counter output LD0 is disambiguated
from the internal register Q0 through its recorded boundary-node identity.
The investigation uses Design's schematic symbols/router with one runtime, a local
camera and a matching retained circuit snapshot. Older records without a matching
snapshot state that limitation. Missing samples remain unavailable.

**Investigation and repair.** The Full Adder journey authors a wrong expectation,
repairs it, replaces an XOR with an OR to create an actual design failure, traces the
failed output into Design, repairs the gate and reruns. The failed recording can be
selected again with its original OR topology, report and observations unchanged.
Recorded explanations use actual prior samples and connected clock/port identities;
unsupported capture semantics and missing setup history are explicitly partial.

**Project depth.** Full recordings and snapshots survive save/reopen and scenario
switches. Two different scenarios retain their own stimulus, selected run, cursor
and representation. Repeated Full Adder instances with matching local labels resolve
to different recorded signals and the correct instance-owned nodes. The circuit
view places actual external neighbours outside the selected instance's recorded
layout; it never edits the historical circuit. Supported bus grouping remains.
The bounded archive retains the latest 50 full recordings.

**Board and Package.** Logical-port assignment and direct compatible-resource
selection are primary. Electrical settings, package pin and XDC consequence appear
with the selected mapping. Bus/batch operations, conflicts, keyboard access and
constraint tools remain. Package opens on state, one generation/download action,
generated file access and the actual Vivado next step. Report is explicit. Draft,
checked, stale and browser download records retain their existing authorities.
The generated-files list describes compiler artifacts; the project ZIP also adds
the `.xpr` project and project-specific setup instructions.
